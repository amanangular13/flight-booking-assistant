Flight Booking Policies

Baggage Policy:
Passengers are generally allowed one cabin bag and one personal item.
Checked baggage allowance depends on the airline and fare type.
Passengers should verify the baggage allowance before booking.

Cancellation Policy:
Cancellation rules depend on the airline and fare type.
Some fares may allow cancellation with a fee.
Some non-refundable fares may not provide a refund.
The assistant should not promise a refund unless the applicable policy confirms it.

Refund Policy:
Eligible refunds are generally processed according to the airline's fare rules.
Refund processing time may vary depending on the payment method and airline.
Refund eligibility should be checked before confirming cancellation.

Check-in:
Passengers should complete online check-in when available.
Check-in opening and closing times may vary by airline and airport.
Passengers should arrive at the airport sufficiently early for security and boarding procedures.

Booking:
A flight should only be considered available when the flight search tool reports available seats.
The assistant must never invent flight availability, prices, flight numbers, or booking confirmation details.

Flight Changes:
Changes to a booking may be subject to airline rules and additional charges.
The assistant should ask for the booking information before discussing a specific booking change.

General Rule:
Flight availability, price, seats, flight numbers, and schedules must come from the flight database through the flight search tool.
General travel policies and informational questions can be answered using the knowledge base.
